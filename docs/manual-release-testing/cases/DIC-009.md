[ ] **DIC-009 — Short dictation tails retain context and failures do not lock other chats** — Using the installed offline speech service in a disposable chat, speak for at least 10 seconds, pause about 500 ms and immediately choose Paste or Send. Repeat at the 30-second hard boundary and with quiet final words. Then exercise a genuine final NoMatch or empty-decode result with unresolved audio. Without choosing Keep for later, edit the recovered text, open a different chat, compose there and explicitly start another dictation. Return to the original chat, open Dictate and retry or confirm discarding the remaining audio. Repeat with delayed microphone closure, a rejected draft write, an unavailable audio-state read and full recovery capacity. Background and resume the app, and repeat old callbacks/actions after the replacement starts. → **Expected:** A short final tail stays with preceding recognition context rather than becoming an isolated subsecond request; all words appear once, Send acts once and native drain remains intact. Explicit completion adds no holdback delay; ongoing capture may wait for two seconds of following audio, with each request within 30 seconds and backlog bounded. Genuinely unrecognized speech still reports truthfully and retains its audio. After confirmed native closure and safe text recovery, the original audio is kept automatically under its account/chat and the app-wide dictation/service owner retires. The composer stays editable and its normal Send remains available; another chat can start a new recording without dismissing an old failure. Remaining audio stays visible only at its origin, and Retry appends the recovered tail there without duplicating text or replaying Send. A blocked explicit Send is still reported as Not sent; no incomplete prefix is submitted automatically. Pending native closure, unsaved text, unknown audio/delivery state, unavailable transfer or full recovery capacity preserve the original data and explain recovery instead of claiming completion. Old callbacks cannot release or stop a new recording, and account/chat removal erases only that origin's kept audio.
